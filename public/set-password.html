🔐 Set your password

Turns a password into the verifier that Cloudflare stores. Do this once, then you can delete this page.

Everything happens in this browser tab. Your password is never sent anywhere — not to Cloudflare, not to this site's server. Only the one-way verifier below leaves your hands, and it cannot be turned back into your password.

  1. Cloudflare dashboard → your todolist-app Worker
  2. Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add
  3. Type: Secret. Name: APP_PASSWORD_HASH
  4. Value: the verifier above. Save, then Deploy.

Then sign in at the app with the password you just chose.
